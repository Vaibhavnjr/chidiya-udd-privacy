Privacy Policy

Chidiya Udd for iPhone · Effective November 20, 2026

Privacy Support

Chidiya Udd is a party game for two to five players on one phone. This policy explains what information the iPhone app handles. The app has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address or phone number.

What we collect

To understand how the game is played and to make it better, the app sends anonymous gameplay events to our analytics provider, PostHog. For example:

  • the app opening, a game starting or ending, and rounds being played
  • the number of players in a game and the round number
  • the app version and build number
  • the device model and iOS version
  • a random ID created when the app is installed, so events from the same install can be grouped

This random ID is not linked to your identity, is not your device's advertising identifier, and is reset if you delete and reinstall the app.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email, phone number or account details
  • No location. The analytics provider is set not to work out a location from your IP address.
  • No contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files. The app plays sounds; it does not listen.
  • No touch positions. Finger positions are used only in memory during a round and are never saved or sent.

How the data is used

The data is used only for analytics: to see which parts of the game people play and where it can improve. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to track you across other apps or websites.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The anonymous events described above contain no personal information.

Your choices

You can stop all data collection at any time by deleting the app. To ask about data from your install, contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example if advertising is added to the game, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

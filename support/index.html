How to play

  • Two to five players each put one finger on the screen.
  • After the countdown, the phone calls out something: "Titli udd!", "Hathi udd!"
  • If that thing can fly, lift your finger. If it can't, keep it down.
  • Get it wrong and you're out. The calls get faster every round, and the last finger left wins.

Common questions

I can't hear the callouts. Turn the volume up and make sure the phone is not on silent mode.

A finger isn't being picked up. Keep fingers a little apart and press with the pad of the finger, not the nail.

Does it need the internet? No. The game plays fully offline.

Does it work on iPad? The game is made for iPhone.
